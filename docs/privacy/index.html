Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

What Niyora Is

Niyora is a macOS menu bar application for guided breathing and mindfulness exercises. Your privacy is important to us, and we've designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

Data We Collect

Local data only. Niyora stores the following on your device:

  • App preferences (window size, mute state)
  • Session completion timestamps (for smart reminders)

This data never leaves your computer. We do not have access to it.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, address)
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No cookies or browser tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No camera or microphone access

Third-Party Services

The Niyora app makes no outbound network requests. It does not include analytics, telemetry, accounts, or any external integrations. Fonts and all other assets are bundled inside the app — nothing about how you use Niyora is sent off your device.

This website only: The Niyora landing page and this privacy page load the Poppins font from Google Fonts. As part of that font request, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. This applies to the website only — not to the Niyora app itself.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling Niyora removes all data. There is nothing to delete on our end because we never had it.

Children's Privacy

Niyora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here with an updated date. If our data practices materially change — for example, if we begin collecting new types of data or sharing data with additional third parties — we will notify users through the app before those changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at support@niyora.com.
